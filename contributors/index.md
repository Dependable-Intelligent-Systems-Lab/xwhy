## Ways to contribute

Contributions can include:

- explainer implementations and tests;
- documentation corrections and new tutorials;
- reproducible worked examples;
- evaluation metrics and benchmark artefacts;
- provider and model integrations;
- accessibility, security, and responsible-use improvements.

Read the [contribution guide](https://dependable-intelligent-systems-lab.github.io/xwhy/contribute/contributing/index.md) before opening a pull request.

## Recognition

Authorship, maintainership, software contribution, and publication authorship are distinct forms of contribution. Repository contributors are generated from GitHub's contributor records. Package authors and maintainers are generated from `pyproject.toml`. Formal research authorship should follow the project's agreed contribution and publication policies.
